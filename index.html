Why Bees Matter?

Bees may be small, but they do a big job. As they fly from flower to flower, they carry pollen. This process called 'pollination', helps plants grow fruits and vegetables. Without bees, many of the foods we eat - like apples, strawberries, and almonds - would become hard to find. Bees also make honey, which people use in cooking and medicine. Sadly bee populations are shrinking due to pesticides, climate change, and fewer flowers in cities. To help bees, we can plant wild flowers, avoid harmful sprays and support bee-friendly farms. Even a small garden can make a difference!
